Relative Chaos

Privacy

Updated September 28, 2026. This covers the Relative Chaos app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Relative Chaos collects nothing, and this version never goes online.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, graphics, controls and controller setups) and your records (wins, knockouts, grudges and the Grudge Book) are kept in the app's own storage. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

The group photo

After a match you can save the group photo. It goes out through your device's own share sheet, to Photos, Files, Messages or wherever you pick. The app only hands over the picture you chose to save.

The internet

This version plays entirely on the device: touch, controllers and computer players. It does not connect to the internet. Online tables and phones as controllers are planned for a later version. Before that update goes out, this page will say exactly what those features send and to where.

Things we do not do

  • No sign in, no email addresses and no payments.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no data sold or shared.
  • No outside services run inside the game. The fonts, pictures, sounds and code all ship inside the app.
  • No special permissions. Where the device has it, the app uses vibration for controller rumble.

Children

Relative Chaos is made for families to play together. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
